# Manager guide

Sign in to the back office at `/login` with your email and password. At a till you use your PIN.

## Every day

**Open the day.** Use **Business day → Open**, or on a till, **End of day** with your PIN. Nobody can sell until the day is open.

**During the day:**

- **Approving with your PIN:** when a cashier asks, type your PIN on their till. You approve:
  - discounts above their limit
  - price changes
  - voids of items the kitchen has
  - refunds
  - no-sales
  - paid in and paid out

  Every approval is recorded with your name. An approval works once, within 2 minutes.

- **Availability:** mark items out of stock; every till updates within seconds.
- **Reports → Today:** sales so far against the same day last week, and live orders.

**Close the day:**

1. Every cashier closes their shift; each one counts their drawer blind.
2. **Business day:** check each shift's counted against expected cash. Differences above the alert amount are highlighted. **Approve** or **Flag** each one with a note.
3. **Close day.** The Z report prints and is kept under **Z reports**.

## Cashiers

**Cashiers:** add a cashier, set their PIN (4–6 digits, different from everyone else's at the branch), reset a forgotten PIN, deactivate someone who leaves. Their history stays.

## Stock

**Stock** shows what's on hand, its value and what's running low (also on your home page).

- **Record waste:** what was thrown away, and why.
- **Transfer stock:** send stock to another branch.
- **Purchase orders:**
  1. Create one (or **Suggest from usage**) and **Mark as sent**.
  2. When the delivery comes, **Receive delivery**: change any line that arrived short or at a different price.
- **Counts:** count the shelf (everything or one category), then **Approve** to set stock to what you counted.
- **Reports:** what the recipes say was used against what actually went, and waste by reason.

## Reports and checks

- **Reports → Sales:**
  - sales by day and by hour
  - product mix
  - channels, order types and payment methods
  - each cashier's sales, voids and refunds
  - speed of service
- **Reports → Tax, Discounts, Promotions.** Every table has **CSV** for Excel.
- **Exceptions:** voids, refunds, discounts and no-sales by cashier and day. Check it daily.
- **Orders:** find any order and see its whole history.
- **Audit log:** every sensitive action at your branch.

## When the internet is down

Tills keep selling and send everything later. The **Sync status** on a till shows what's waiting; you can retry or discard stuck items. Shifts and the day can't close until everything is sent.
