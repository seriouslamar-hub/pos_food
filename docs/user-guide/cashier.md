# Cashier quick guide

## Start of shift

1. Tap your **PIN** on the till.
2. If the till says the business day isn't open, ask a manager. They open it from **End of day** on the till (with their PIN) or from the back office.
3. **Count your float:** enter how many of each note and coin are in the drawer, then **Open shift**.

## Taking an order

1. Pick **Dine in**, **Takeaway** or **Delivery** at the top of the order. Delivery needs the customer's phone number and address.
2. Tap a category, then the item. Items with choices (sizes, extras, meals) open a sheet: pick what the customer wants. Meals ask for each part in turn (side, drink, upsize).
3. In the order on the right:
   - **+ / −** changes how many.
   - Tap a line to change its choices or add a note ("no pepper").
   - **⋯** on a line gives it a discount or a different price. Above your limit, a manager approves with their PIN.
   - **Discount** takes something off the whole order.
4. Greyed-out items are out of stock.
5. **Hold** parks the order with a name; **Recall** brings it back.

### Promotions, coupons and loyalty

- Deals that are running (lunch specials, meal deals) apply by themselves and show in green in the order.
- **Coupon:** type the customer's code. If the order doesn't qualify yet, it says so.
- **Customer:** type their phone number and tap **Find**.
  - **New customers:** ask whether they want to join. Only tick "agrees" if they say yes; their number is never kept without it.
  - **Spending points:** type how many (or **All**) and tap **Use this customer**.
  - **Earning:** points are added when the order is paid.

## Kiosk and online orders

Orders from the self-order kiosk, and online orders the customer chose to pay at the counter, wait under **Unpaid orders** (marked Kiosk or Online). When the customer comes to pay, tap **Take** on their order and take payment as usual; it then goes to the kitchen. Online orders paid online go straight to the kitchen. The kitchen ticket shows "Online" and the pickup time.

## Taking payment

1. **Charge** opens the payment screen.
2. **Cash:** tap a quick amount or type what the customer gave you. The till shows the change.
3. **Card or mobile money:** take the payment on the terminal or phone, then type its reference.
4. **Split:** pay part by one method, then the rest by another, until nothing is left to pay.
5. The receipt prints (or tap **Print receipt**). **Reprint last receipt** prints a copy marked COPY.

If the total changes when you charge, the server worked out a different price (for example, a menu price changed). Charge what the till shows.

## Mistakes and refunds

- **Remove an item before charging:** tap **−** or remove the line.
- **Void items on an unpaid order:** **Orders** → find it → **Void items**. Items the kitchen already has need a manager's PIN.
- **Refund:** **Orders** → find it by number, amount or payment reference → **Refund** → everything or some items → how the money goes back → a reason. A manager approves with their PIN.
- Tick **Put the items back in stock** only if they weren't made or are sealed.

## When the internet is down

The till keeps selling.

- **Offline orders:** they get numbers like **T1-042**, and a kitchen ticket prints for each one.
- **What waits for the connection:** card and mobile money (unless your manager allows them offline), refunds, voids, coupons and loyalty.
- **The badge at the top** shows how many sales are waiting to be sent. They go by themselves when the connection comes back.
- **Don't close your shift** until the badge is gone; the till won't let you.

## End of shift

1. **Close my shift.** Count the drawer note by note. You won't see what the till expects; that's normal.
2. A manager checks the count.
3. **X report** shows your shift so far at any time.

## Training mode

If the till shows **TRAINING MODE**, nothing you do counts. Practise freely: sales, refunds, cash. Receipts say "TRAINING · NOT A RECEIPT".
